## Method

The extraction uses the rendered Angular DOM in the saved HTML. Source URLs are not links in that DOM; they were recovered from `kMDItemWhereFroms` metadata on saved favicon sidecars. Those URLs were not visited or validated.

The in-app browser rejected the local `file://` URL under its browser safety policy. Computer Use was not used as a workaround. Direct local parsing supplied the structured evidence needed for this documentation.
