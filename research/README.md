# Research

Research records evidence, methods, findings, examples, and limitations. It is
not loaded wholesale by derived capabilities.

- [`readme-anatomy-v1.md`](readme-anatomy-v1.md) synthesizes the current domain
  model and authoring implications.
- [`readme-anatomy-v1-sources.md`](readme-anatomy-v1-sources.md) records sources
  by the claims they can support.
- [`readme-anatomy-v1-repository-watchlist.md`](readme-anatomy-v1-repository-watchlist.md)
  keeps exploratory and user-preferred examples separate from promoted
  evidence.

Research conclusions become operational guidance only after their scope and
decision effect are clear. Capabilities should cite a domain version or source
revision rather than embedding an untraceable second research narrative.
