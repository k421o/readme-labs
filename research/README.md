# Research

Research records evidence, methods, findings, examples, and limitations. It is
not loaded wholesale by derived capabilities.

- [`readme-anatomy-v1.md`](readme-anatomy-v1.md) synthesizes the current domain
  model and authoring implications.
- [`readme-anatomy-v1-sources.md`](readme-anatomy-v1-sources.md) records sources
  by the claims they can support.
- [`readme-anatomy-v1-repository-watchlist.md`](readme-anatomy-v1-repository-watchlist.md)
  keeps exploratory and user-preferred examples separate from promoted
  evidence.
- [`readme-purpose.md`](readme-purpose.md) records the earlier delivery-contract
  research that separated README surfaces from ambient agent instructions.
- [`anatomy-of-a-readme/`](anatomy-of-a-readme/) and
  [`common-readme-pitfalls/`](common-readme-pitfalls/) preserve focused findings
  imported by the concurrent `v0.1.0` extraction.
- [`readme-contract-exercises/`](readme-contract-exercises/) preserves three
  blind reconstruction exercises as historical evaluation evidence. Their
  source links and score material are intentionally visible, so they are not
  held-out scenarios for current efficacy claims.

Research conclusions become operational guidance only after their scope and
decision effect are clear. Capabilities should cite a domain version or source
revision rather than embedding an untraceable second research narrative.

The [concurrent bootstrap reconciliation](../docs/migrations/2026-08-30-concurrent-bootstrap-reconciliation.md)
records why both import paths exist and how overlapping artifacts were
classified.
