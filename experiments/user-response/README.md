# User-response research

README Labs may study whether people can identify a project, choose the right
path, complete a task, navigate to deeper documentation, and understand
important boundaries. No single methodology is canonical.

A checked-in observation records only a stable envelope: study and artifact
identity, the versioned method, collection time, participant scope, privacy
classification, consent basis when applicable, and an open method-defined
payload. Surveys, interviews, moderated tasks, unmoderated usability runs, and
aggregate product telemetry may therefore evolve independently.

Do not commit direct identifiers, private recordings, raw credentials, or
sensitive free-form responses. Keep those in an approved protected store and
record only a durable reference and permitted derivative here.
